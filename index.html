Your heart

Progress that
only rises.

Every step you take adds Health Points, and they fill a heart from the bottom up. A walk after lunch counts. Two minutes around the block counts too.

  • It never empties. When a colour is full, the next one rises over the colour you’ve earned.
  • Sixteen colours, sage to gold. Gold is simply the colour that takes a lifetime of small steps.
  • This week, made visible. A lighter band shows what you added in the last seven days.

Health Points count actions, not years. Centen promises nothing; it suggests actions where the evidence favours a benefit to your health.

The Centen token

A promise you
can wear.

A small token on a woven band. It shows what you have done, makes today’s plan a promise and tells you when it’s time, without asking you to look at your phone.

  1. 01

    It shows what you’ve done

    Every time you see it, it reminds you that you invest in your health. It changes as you act: new light patterns first, later an engraving. Wearing it and buying it earn nothing; only your actions do.

  2. 02

    One press makes it a promise

    When today’s plan is ready, the token glows softly. Press it once to accept the plan, and once more when it’s done. The commitment comes from the gesture, not the electronics.

  3. 03

    A cue without the screen

    When the moment is right, a short vibration that you learn to recognise tells you it’s time. At most twice a day, and never during your quiet hours.

The token shows what you have done. It does not do it for you.

Why this today?
